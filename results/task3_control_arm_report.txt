==============================================================================

(R-47's inference) or merely from having 195 more windows?

  duplication seeds: [10000, 10001, 10002]

-- verdict -----------------------------------------------------------------
  R-47's mechanism is CONFIRMED. 195 perfectly fittable duplicate windows
  cost nothing measurable; 195 spliced windows cost ~21%. The rise is caused
  by splice content, not by dataset size.
