# Submission venue — the MLRC 2026 question, checked

The revision brief asked whether the expression-of-interest path to MLRC 2026 changes the
submission plan. **It does not, and the reason is now definite rather than estimated.**

Checked 23 August 2026 against `reproml.org` and the NeurIPS 2026 call for reproducibility.

## What MLRC 2026 requires

MLRC 2026 is an official NeurIPS track for 2026. It does not accept direct submissions of
reproducibility papers; it draws from **TMLR-accepted** ones. Three routes in:

| route | deadline | open to us? |
|---|---|---|
| Expression of Interest, while under review at TMLR | **4 June 2026** | **no — passed** |
| Self-nomination, after TMLR acceptance | acceptance must be in the system by **30 Sept 2026** | no |
| Area Chair nomination | same window | no |

Eligibility additionally requires the paper to be accepted **as is or with minor revisions** to
TMLR, with the acceptance recorded between 20 June 2025 and 30 September 2026. Author
notifications for the track were 7 October 2026.

## Why neither route is reachable

The EOI deadline passed on 4 June 2026, eleven weeks before this revision was finished, so the
"express interest while under review" route is closed regardless of when we submit.

The remaining routes need a TMLR **acceptance decision** — not a submission — logged by
30 September 2026. Submitting in late August against TMLR's two-month target for a first
decision leaves no room for the revision round that "accepted with minor revisions" implies. It
is not a close call.

## The decision

**Submit to TMLR on TMLR's own clock, and do not shape the paper around an MLRC deadline that
cannot be met.** Nothing about the work changes: the reproducibility certification TMLR issues
stands on its own, and MLRC eligibility was never a criterion any of the analysis was built for.

If the paper is accepted to TMLR after 30 September 2026, the self-nomination route remains open
for a **future** MLRC cycle, and the acceptance date will simply fall in that cycle's window.
That is worth remembering at acceptance time; it is not worth optimising for now.

## What this does not affect

Nothing in `PAPER.md`. This is a venue-logistics record, not a claim, and it is deliberately kept
out of the paper and out of `FINDINGS_LEDGER.md`, which is for claims about the reproduction.
