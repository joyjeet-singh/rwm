## The gate

Annex 2 X5 runs the rule only if the command-tracking terms (`track_lin_vel_xy_exp`, `track_ang_vel_z_exp`) qualify. They do not: each needs the command (`base_velocity`), which the environment samples and the data do not hold. **X5 is NOT RUN.** No reward subset is defined. Inferring the command from the recorded base velocity would not make it recorded data. It would also make the tracking reward of the recorded trajectory high by construction, since the command would be fitted to the very velocities the term scores. The terms that would qualify on their own (`lin_vel_z_l2`, `ang_vel_xy_l2`, `dof_torques_l2`, `action_rate_l2`, `undesired_contacts`, `flat_orientation_l2`, `dof_pos_limits`) carry none of the reward's purpose, which is to follow the command, and Annex 2 does not allow a subset without the tracking terms.
